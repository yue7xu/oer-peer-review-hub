HOW PEER REVIEW WORKS

The peer review flow, with every role on one path.

Trained reviewers evaluate each resource against six evidence-based rubrics, criterion by criterion. Authors respond and can revise, and every result you see is transparent down to the criterion.

Trace one role through the flow
A coordinator matches reviewers and keeps rounds on schedule behind the scenes. Coordinators never appear in a public review.
Reviewer 01

Reviewers qualify first

Every reviewer completes a pre-training course before they can review, so each evaluation comes from a qualified, calibrated reviewer.

  • A structured course on the six rubrics and how to apply them
  • Only qualified reviewers can review and earn a badge
Author 02

Submit the resource

An author adds an OER link and picks the rubrics it should be reviewed on.

  • Links to where the resource already lives
  • The author sets whether the review can become public
Reviewer 03

Review against the rubrics

A matched reviewer works through the resource criterion by criterion across the six rubrics, backed by evidence at every point.

  • One rating and comment per criterion
  • Reviewers are anonymous in public reviews

  • Unpublish or re-publish whenever they choose

Public 07

  • Ratings and comments, criterion by criterion
  • Private and student reviews never appear here
THE RUBRIC METHOD

Single-point rubrics, built for comments.

All six rubrics share one design. Each criterion describes a single target — what meeting the standard looks like — and reviewers respond with specific written comments instead of a number. A criterion isn't forced into one verdict: the same one can be marked as exceeding in some ways and needing work in others, at the same time.
